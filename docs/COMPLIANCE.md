## 6. Permitted Alternatives (if licences cannot be provided)

If valid RTA/ITC transport permits cannot be produced, the following are lawful options:

(a) **Licensed partner model:** site promotes a licensed operator; MJ acts as a disclosed lead/dispatch agent. Relationship must be stated clearly on the site.

(b) **Informational site only:** route guides, official travel options (RTA buses, Careem, Uber, Hala), no sale of unlicensed rides.

(c) **Other licensed activity:** site covers only the activity listed on the actual trade licence.

Owner must choose one of the above before Phase 1 proceeds if transport permits are absent.
